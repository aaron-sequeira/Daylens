Pop-up manners (built in, adjustable in Settings)

  • ⏱At most 1 every 20 min, except break reminders you've asked for. Anything held back goes into a “while you were busy” summary.
  • 🎮Silent when it matters: full-screen apps, games, video calls (Zoom, Meet, Teams), screen sharing, and Windows Do Not Disturb.
  • ✋Learns from you: if you dismiss the same kind of pop-up three times, it backs off and asks whether you want fewer.
  • 🔕Snooze options: 10 min, 1 hour, or rest of today. Each of the 4 types can be switched off on its own.
  • ⌨Never steals focus: pop-ups don't grab your keyboard, so typing never lands in them. Win+Shift+D dismisses them all.
