Delete your Daily account

App: Daily (com.aaronalex.daily) · Developer: Aaron Alex Sequeira

How to request deletion

  1. If you have a Daily Plus subscription, cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Daily.
  2. Email aaronalexsequeira@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Daily account, from the email address you signed in with (your Google or email account). If you never signed in, include the account ID shown in Daily → Settings.
  3. We delete your account and the data below within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Delete your data without deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Delete everything to erase all history stored on your phone, or Settings → Export my data to keep a copy first. Uninstalling Daily also removes everything on the phone. Settings → Account → Sign out disconnects your account from the phone.
